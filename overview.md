# Cuvola at a Glance

## Who we are

Cuvola (koo-VOH-lah) is a culinary technology venture within GV / [Genoventures](https://genoventures.com/). We create independent products for people who cook, learn, and work in kitchens.

Cuvola is the company, not a single application or a mandatory product suite. It is not exclusively an AI venture: the right tool may use conventional software, AI, or a combination of both.

The name is coined; it is not a claimed Italian translation.

## Our purpose

**Make cooking better through useful technology.**

We want to help people learn, experiment, and cook with more confidence. Technology should support the experience, not take the cook out of it.

## Our ambition

A world where everyone can cook with confidence, creativity, and joy, from home kitchens to professional settings. Our current audience focus is home cooks and chefs at home; that broader ambition is not a promise to serve every kitchen at once.

## Our venture tagline

> A new way forward for every kitchen.
