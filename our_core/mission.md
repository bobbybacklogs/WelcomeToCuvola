# Cuvola Core: Mission and Vision

## Mission statement

**Make cooking better through useful technology.**

We build practical tools that help people plan, prepare, cook, and learn. That includes understanding ingredients, choosing equipment, developing technique, managing timing, and adapting when a meal doesn't go to plan.

Our aim is to make cooking more accessible and enjoyable without overshadowing the craft. Clear guidance, useful assistance, and reliable tools should help the cook make decisions, not make them feel dependent on technology.

## Vision statement

**A world where everyone can cook with confidence, creativity, and joy.**

We want to earn a meaningful place in culinary technology through practical tools, useful assistance, and enjoyable learning. Our long-term ambition reaches from home kitchens to professional settings, while our current work focuses on home cooks and chefs at home.

Cooking is a skill people can keep developing. We want our products to encourage curiosity, experimentation, and the satisfaction of getting better.

## Values in practice

- **Accessibility:** Make tools and guidance approachable for different skill levels.
- **Reliability:** Be clear about what a product can do and where its limits are.
- **Curiosity:** Encourage learning and thoughtful experimentation.
- **Respect for the craft:** Use technology to support culinary skill, not replace its substance.

Our [values](values.md) explain how these principles guide product choices, education, and business commitments.
