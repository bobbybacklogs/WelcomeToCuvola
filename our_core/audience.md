# Cuvola Core: Audience

## Our current focus

**Home cooks and chefs at home.** We want to make planning, preparing, and cooking a meal easier to understand and manage, while helping people build practical skills.

Within that focus, we're thinking about:

- **Beginners:** People learning the basics who want clear guidance and confidence.
- **Families:** Households balancing meals, different needs, and shared kitchen responsibilities.
- **Home cooks:** People who cook regularly and want useful tools for everyday meals and continued learning.
- **Chefs at home:** Experienced home cooks who enjoy refining technique and taking on more involved dishes.
- **Food enthusiasts:** Curious cooks who enjoy exploring ingredients, recipes, and culinary traditions.

These groups overlap. Each product still needs a specific audience and a clear problem to solve.

## Longer-term possibilities

Cuvola's broader ambition includes professional and educational settings:

- **Independent restaurants:** Smaller operations looking for practical support in the kitchen.
- **Larger kitchen operations:** Teams with more complex coordination and workflow needs.
- **Culinary educators:** People helping others develop cooking skills and understanding.

These are possible future audiences, not a commitment that current products serve them. Expansion should follow evidence of a useful product fit, rather than trying to build for every kitchen at once.
