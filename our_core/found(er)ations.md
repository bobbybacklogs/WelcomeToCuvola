# The Found(er)ations of Cuvola

Founder Cassian Wolfe is Sicilian-American, with a sales background and a self-taught cooking journey through books, YouTube and curiosity-led practice. His relationship with cooking includes childhood kitchen memories with his nana and mom, including biscuits. Do not invent formal culinary credentials or present him as a professionally trained chef.

Learning influences include Cullinary Institute Of America(self-taught), J. Kenji López-Alt,Chef Jean-Pierre, Jacques Pépin, Tom Colicchio and Jacob Burton / Stella Culinary, including F-STEP. They inform an interest in preparation, technique, judgment and entertaining teaching. They are not partners, endorsers or licensed Cuvola instructors.

Cuvola’s philosophy emphasizes practical skill-building, enjoyment in the kitchen, and the cultivation of personal culinary confidence, rather than formal culinary accolades or professional credentials.

The brand identity reflects this philosophy, combining a warm, approachable aesthetic with a focus on real-world culinary skills and experiences. It is designed to resonate with home cooks who value learning, creativity, and the joy of cooking over formal recognition.

Cuvola’s approach to product development mirrors this ethos, prioritizing user-centric design, practical functionality, and the enhancement of everyday cooking experiences. Each product is conceived as a tool to empower home cooks, fostering confidence and enjoyment in the kitchen rather than emphasizing technical prestige or professional validation.

### From Cassian, the Founder of Cuvola,

Cuvola was born out of my personal journey in the kitchen — a journey fueled by curiosity, self-teaching, and a deep appreciation for the joy of cooking. While Chef's and culinary professionals have their own formal tools in the kitchen, I wanted to bring them the same sense of hands-on empowerment through agent-driven culinary technology that supports learning, experimentation, and confidence for not only professional chefs but also home cooks of all skill levels. My goal is to create an environment where anyone can feel capable and inspired to cook, regardless of their prior experience or background.

I envision Cuvola as a bridge between traditional culinary expertise and modern technological innovation, providing intuitive tools that enhance the cooking experience without overshadowing the personal touch and creativity that make home cooking so special. By focusing on practical skill-building, enjoyment, and personal growth in the kitchen, I aim to cultivate a community of empowered home cooks who can confidently explore and express their culinary creativity.

Ultimately, my aspiration is for Cuvola to become a trusted companion in every home kitchen, helping individuals discover the joy of cooking, develop their skills, and create memorable culinary experiences for themselves and their loved ones.
