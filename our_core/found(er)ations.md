# The Found(er)ations of Cuvola

## The founder's background

Cassian Wolfe is Sicilian-American, with a background in sales and a self-taught cooking journey built on books, YouTube, and curiosity-led practice. His relationship with food also reaches back to childhood kitchen memories with his nana and mom, including biscuits.

His learning influences include self-study drawing on the Culinary Institute of America, J. Kenji López-Alt, Chef Jean-Pierre, Jacques Pépin, Tom Colicchio, and Jacob Burton / Stella Culinary, including F-STEP. Those influences inform his interest in preparation, technique, judgment, and teaching that is both useful and entertaining.

> **Accuracy note:** Do not imply formal culinary training or enrollment, or present Cassian as a professionally trained chef. The people and institutions named here are learning influences, not partners, endorsers, or licensed Cuvola instructors.

## What that means for Cuvola

Cuvola values practical skills, enjoyment, and personal confidence over culinary credentials or technical prestige. That carries through to the brand and its products: warm, approachable tools that help people get more out of everyday cooking.

## From Cassian, the founder

Cuvola started with my own time in the kitchen: learning, trying things, making mistakes, and discovering how much I enjoy cooking. I'm self-taught, and I know the difference a clear explanation or the right tool can make when you're figuring something out.

I want to bring that kind of practical help to more kitchens. Whether you're learning the basics or already know your way around a stove, you should have tools that help you experiment, build skills, and feel more capable.

For me, technology belongs in service of that experience. It should make cooking easier to understand and more enjoyable, not get between you and the food, or take away the creativity and personal touch that make it yours.

My hope is that Cuvola earns a place in people's kitchens by being genuinely useful: helping them grow, enjoy the process, and make meals worth sharing with the people they love.
