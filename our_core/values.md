# Cuvola Core: Values

## Our Values

Commit to the culinary world beyond any individual product. Learn from experiments, including products that do not work.
Let enjoyment and curiosity drive exploration. Use evidence to guide business commitments, investment, pricing and launch promises.
Keep products independent, with their own audiences and purposes.
Use deterministic software where appropriate. AI is optional across the venture and belongs where it adds useful value.
Make education genuinely entertaining while preserving culinary substance and safety.
Keep the cook in control. Favor clear next actions, relevant context and honest limits.

Foster a community of cooks who share knowledge, experiences, and inspiration, while maintaining respect for diverse culinary traditions and practices.

---

## Summary

Cuvola's values emphasize a commitment to the culinary world, the importance of curiosity and enjoyment in exploration, and the independence of our products. We prioritize the use of deterministic software and the optional integration of AI where it adds value. Education is designed to be both entertaining and substantive, while the cook remains in control throughout the culinary journey. Finally, we foster a respectful and collaborative community of cooks, ensuring that diverse culinary traditions and practices are honored.
