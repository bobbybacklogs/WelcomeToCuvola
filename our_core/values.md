# Cuvola Core: Values

- **Commit to cooking, not just a product.** Stay invested in the culinary world. Learn from experiments, including products that do not work.
- **Explore with curiosity; commit with evidence.** Let enjoyment drive exploration. Use evidence to guide investment, pricing, and launch promises.
- **Give each product its own purpose.** Keep products independent, with their own audiences and reasons to exist.
- **Use the right technology.** Choose deterministic software where appropriate. AI is optional across the venture and belongs where it adds useful value.
- **Make learning enjoyable and substantive.** Education can be entertaining without compromising culinary substance or safety.
- **Keep the cook in control.** Favor clear next actions, relevant context, and honest limits.
- **Learn together.** Encourage cooks to share knowledge, experiences, and inspiration, with respect for diverse culinary traditions and practices.
