# Typography Guidelines

The fonts below are recommendations, not confirmed selections or an identification of the lettering in the artwork. Final choices and licensing remain to be confirmed.

## Primary Display / Wordmark (Serif)

* **Style:** Expressive serif with warmth and a strong display presence (e.g., *Ogg, Freight Display, Recoleta, or Playfair Display*).
* **Usage:** Brand name, headline slogans, editorial titles, awards, and pull quotes.
* **Characteristics:** Elegance, heritage, warmth, literary weight.

## Subtitle / Secondary Typography

* **Tagline Typography:** Refined classic serif (e.g., *Baskerville, EB Garamond, or Cormorant Garamond*).
* **Usage:** Subheaders and supporting statement lockups (*"The technology is the work. The people are the reason."*).

## Body & Functional Copy

* **Typeface Recommendation:** Clear, readable interface type (e.g., *Ideal Sans, Optima, Lato, or Inter*).
* **Usage:** App interfaces, packaging ingredient decks, body copy, technical instructions, web documentation.
* **Rule:** Ensure ample line height (around 1.5–1.6 times the font size) and high contrast against Tuscan cream or espresso backgrounds.
* **Contrast Rule:** Avoid low-contrast text on textured or patterned backgrounds to maintain readability and accessibility.
* **Accessibility Rule:** Ensure text meets WCAG contrast standards for readability, especially on colored or textured backgrounds.
